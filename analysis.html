ICP: Large and multinational employers, typically 5,000 plus staff, across financial services, technology, pharma and life sciences, retail, industrials and the public sector, that already run Workday or SAP SuccessFactors and are moving to skills-based talent decisions. Personas: CHRO, Chief People Officer, VP Talent Acquisition, Head of Talent and People Analytics, Head of Internal Mobility, and the CIO or Head of HR Technology. Drivers: the enterprise shift to skills-based organisation and internal mobility, the EU AI Act treatment of recruitment AI as high risk, cost pressure on hiring and retention, and the platform-to-PaaS shift that rewards land and expand.
